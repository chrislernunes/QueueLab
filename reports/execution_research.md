## What this does not show

- No live Binance depth is in the committed dataset. Public historical dumps do not include the incremental depth required to rebuild this book. A capture has to be taken prospectively and is not redistributed here.
- Queue position is not observed. Model differences are assumptions.
- Latency numbers in the grid are not measured round-trip times.
- The fee is 100 ppm in the session config, not a Binance fee-tier lookup.
- Positive spread capture can coexist with negative markout. The table shows both.
